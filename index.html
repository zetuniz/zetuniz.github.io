Young. Hungry. Building the Future.

I'm Thomas — an ICT student and self-driven digital creator from Zimbabwe, building real-world products that make an impact. What started as curiosity about how websites work has grown into a full-stack creative practice spanning web development, graphic design, AI automation, and business systems.

I don't just write code — I craft experiences. Whether it's a sleek restaurant website, an AI-powered assistant, or a brand identity that stands out, I bring the same obsessive attention to detail to every pixel and every line.

Currently building ZetuMarket, an African marketplace platform, and Zeno AI, a smart assistant for small businesses. I'm always open to exciting freelance projects, collabs, and startup opportunities.
