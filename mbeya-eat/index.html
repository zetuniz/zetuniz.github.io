Our Story

Born from the Heart of Mbeya

Mbeya Eat was born in 2016 from a simple dream: to create a place where the rich flavors of Tanzania meet modern dining excellence. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into Mbeya's most beloved restaurant destination.

Our founder, Mama Amara, believed that food is more than nourishment — it is culture, memory, and connection. Every dish we serve carries her philosophy: "Cook with love, serve with pride."

Meet the Maestro

The Chef Behind the Magic

Best Chef 2023

Chef Amara Mwangi

Executive Head Chef & Co-Founder

With over 20 years of culinary experience across Tanzania, Kenya, and France, Chef Amara brings an extraordinary blend of East African tradition and international technique to every dish. Trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris, she returned to her hometown of Mbeya to create something truly special.
